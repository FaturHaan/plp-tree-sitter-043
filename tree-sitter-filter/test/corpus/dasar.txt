==================

---

(query

==================
Ekspresi dalam tanda kurung
==================

(query
  (and_expression

          field: (identifier)
          value: (number))
        right: (comparison
          field: (identifier)
          value: (number))))
    right: (comparison
      field: (identifier)
      value: (number))))
